Help & Information

About QuantVAT

Quantitative Crypto Volume Analysis Tool is an ecosystem built for data-driven traders. It tracks volumetric activity and performs cross-market analysis by fusing Spot and Futures data.

It also features the Deep Diver Engine for advanced spot analysis and a semi-automated Trading Journal with blunt AI auditing.

Quick Start Guide

  1. Register & Login: Create your account. It is easy, fast, and free.
  2. Setup Wizard: Configure your API keys (CoinGeko and VTMR URL) once.
  3. Spot Scan: Tap to Identify volume-weighted spot tokens from the last 24 hours.
  4. Futures Data: Export the PDF from CoinAlyze and upload it here.
  5. Cross-Analysis: The engine fuses the data and generates your OISS score.
  6. Report: Download your professional PDF report instantly.
